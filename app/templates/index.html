Authentication & Spoofing Prevention

Every account is bound to a unique RSA-2048 key pair. Outgoing emails are signed to guarantee they originate from the true sender, rendering address spoofing impossible.

Data Integrity via Hashing

Emails generate a unique SHA-256 hash. If even a single character is tampered with during transmission, signature verification fails and flags the message immediately.

Certificate Authority (CA)

A simulated central CA validates and signs users' public keys with X.509 standard certificates, mitigating any Man-in-the-Middle impersonation risks.
